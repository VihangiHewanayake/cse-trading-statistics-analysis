# Annual Trading Statistics of the Colombo Stock Exchange

## Project Overview
This project focuses on the statistical and exploratory analysis of annual trading statistics of selected companies listed in the Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE) from 2006–2022.

The analysis examines turnover behavior, trading activity, price volatility, and market phase dynamics using statistical modeling and data visualization techniques.

---

## Objectives
- Analyze long-term trading behavior in the Colombo Stock Exchange
- Examine relationships between turnover, trade volume, share volume, and volatility
- Identify structural market phases and trading patterns
- Apply statistical inference and regression modeling techniques

---

## Key Findings
- Trading turnover exhibited non-linear and phase-based behavior
- Strong relationships existed between trading activity and turnover
- Structural market shocks significantly influenced trading patterns
- Volatility relationships varied across companies and market conditions
